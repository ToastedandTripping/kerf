# Kerf v0.8.32: owner test cards

Run these at the Mac on the installed v0.8.32 build. Cards 1 and 2 use the laser, Card 3 does not. Kerf can only report what it commanded, so each card's evidence is what you see on the card stock or on the panel.

## Before any burn

Keep the extraction running and a fire extinguisher or blanket within reach, and stay at the machine for every burn. Find the switch that cuts power to the laser module (expected: the machine's main power inlet). If you can't identify it with confidence, don't burn. If anything goes wrong, press STOP and then cut that switch, because Kerf can't confirm the beam is off and the switch can.

## Card 1: image curves no longer burn a negative

Scrap card on the bed with nothing burnable under it.

1. Download the test project from https://github.com/ToastedandTripping/kerf/raw/master/docs/owner-cards/tb3-legacy-s-curve.kerf and open it in Kerf. Don't save it at any point.
2. Check the Engrave layer before anything else: Variable, Max 15%, Min 0, 3000 mm/min, Grayscale, 0.1 mm interval, 1 pass, image adjustments neutral. If any value differs, stop.
3. Press Generate, then FRAME, and watch the head trace a 40 x 10 mm box. Engrave it as it is. This is the old curve, so the white end should burn darkest. It shows old projects were left alone.
   If this leaves no mark at all, raise Max to 20% and use 20% for every burn on this card. Never go above 20% or slower than 3000 mm/min. If 20% still leaves nothing, write down "inconclusive" and stop.
4. Slide the card so clean stock sits under the frame. Open the Engrave layer's power curve, pick S-Curve and press Apply. Press Generate and check the button reads "Generate G-code", not "Regenerate". FRAME, then engrave. Pass: the black end is darkest, the white end is unmarked, and the shade fades smoothly between them.
5. Slide the card again. Pick Posterize, press Apply, then Generate, FRAME and engrave. Pass: three distinct bands from dark to light, with the white end unmarked.

Result: passed, failed, or inconclusive. Keep the card.

## Card 2: jog safety

Laser module power isolated for the whole card, so nothing here burns. The arrows jog at 1000 mm/min, and the only moves on this card are Home and 1 mm steps.

Before starting, confirm the panel shows `$21=1` (hard limits) and `$13=0` (millimetres). If it doesn't, write down "inconclusive" and stop. Then check once that switching the controller off stops a Home within a second.

1. Send `$1=255` from the console, then `$$`, and confirm it reads `$1=255`. Leave the machine idle for ten minutes and touch each motor. Warm is fine. If one is too hot to hold, send `$1=250`, note it, and never push the head by hand after a Home.
2. Connect. The arrows are blocked and the panel asks for a Home.
3. Press Home. While it is homing, press an arrow. It is refused, and nothing moves afterwards.
4. Set the step to 1 mm. With a ruler, check there is at least 10 mm of clearance on both sides of each axis. Jog +X, -X, +Y and -Y once each. Every press moves 1 mm, and the panel position changes by 1.000 each time.
5. Check for 60 mm of clearance on +X and 20 mm on -X. Press +X twenty times as fast as the button allows. The panel X should end exactly 20.000 further on, within 0.01. Twenty presses of -X bring it back. Any press Kerf refused shows a reason and never queues a move.
6. From the console send `$J=G21 G91 X1 F100` and press an arrow straight away. It is refused until the head has stopped.
7. From the console send `$J=G21 G91 X1 F20`, a three-second move, and press STOP partway through. The arrows ask for a Home. Send `$X`, and they still ask for a Home until you press it.
8. Unplug the USB cable and plug it back in. The arrows ask for a Home until you press it.
9. Leave the machine connected and idle for five minutes, then run any job of two minutes or more with the laser still isolated. There should be no "Connection lost" at any point.

Result: passed, or the number of the step that failed.

## Card 3: desktop checks, no laser

1. File > Open, pick any PDF that has line art in it, and import it once with Raster selected and once with Vector. Both should land on the canvas. If either shows "Failed to load PDF", note it, because PDF support was updated in this release and only the Mac's own browser engine can confirm it.
2. Connected and idle, STOP is a red outline. During any job it becomes the large solid red button, and nothing on the job bar is cut off.
3. Draw a rectangle, press Generate, and change the layer's speed before it finishes. The button should then read "Regenerate G-code" and START should refuse. If generation finishes too fast to catch, skip this step.
