## Overall verdict

**FAIL** — on core 3 (Completeness), with X1 and X5 at CONCERN. The batch's design is right and its two departures from the parent are improvements the tree confirms (FRAME keeps `gcodeStale`; `$N` invalidates). Separately, the verification section would not survive contact with the battery as written: two mutants cannot be killed through a disabled button, one console prefix reds the baseline, option (a) reds a second existing test, and "controls green" is not an outcome the journal can record. Each is hours; together they are why this is a FAIL and not a CONCERN — an X1 batch whose central claim is false and whose battery cannot run clean should not dispatch until the plan says how both are made true.
