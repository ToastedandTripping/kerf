### 2. Approach soundness — **PASS**
Checked against stock GRBL source, not the parent's prose. (a) Gating the hold auto-off on `$32`: stock `protocol.c` raises `EXEC_SPINDLE_OVR_STOP` at hold-complete only under `bit_istrue(settings.flags, BITFLAG_LASER_MODE)`, so "under `$32=0` the spindle stays on through the hold" is the stock model, and it errs on the dangerous side, which is the right side for a fixture the pause path is tested against. The existing `0x9E` toggle then falls out correctly for both cases without edit (spindle on in Hold + `0x9E` → off, which is stock's non-laser behaviour). (b) A settings *table* with `laser_mode()` derived from it, rather than relay-plan D1's `laser_mode: bool` field, is the better shape: one source, and E5-M1 can only be killed by a dump that reads the table. (c) Departure 1 is correct: a Stock-only "`A:S` iff spindle on" model tested green would assert a protocol the capture contradicts at lines 70, 83, 212 and 214; splitting it by profile and refusing to model the refresh cycle is honest. (d) Departure 2 is correct: `serial_pump.rs`'s test module has no sim in it, and the two existing tests the wedge tests are modelled on both live in `sim_integration`. (e) The wedge as "accepted, executed, never acked, `?` answers" with the arming line itself unacked matches DECISIONS 2026-09-05's description and the probe's message shape; the plan says plainly it is a modelling choice pending session A. Executing the `M4` (so `spindle_on` is true during the wedge) is the conservative choice: any stop tested against it must clear the beam through `0x18`.
