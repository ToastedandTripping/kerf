## Three stress tests

**Pre-mortem (three months out, it failed).**
1. *The dialogs open on the close button.* A3 shipped the shell; A4, A5 and A11 migrated eleven dialogs, each passing `initialFocus` "as today", which for most was nothing. Every dialog now opens with focus on the dismissing control; Enter closes the dialog the operator just opened. The browser recording in A3 compared Tab order and missed the initial focus because the recorder started after the first Tab. We should have seen it in the shell's spec: a focusable element placed before the body changes the trap's default.
2. *A11 stalls or quietly edits a safety test.* The "Start test" rename breaks seven assertions in `machineJobLoop.test.tsx`; the relay, told the suite must pass unmodified, either stops or edits the test outside the mutation discipline. The second is worse: that file is the behavioural evidence that no handler moved in A7.
3. *The Fire label is the one sentence an operator remembers.* "Fire (0.5 s pulse)" ships; after a job the controller is G0-modal and nothing marks; the operator raises power or presses again, or assumes the laser is dead. Type-specific worst case: an operator who trusts the label walks a hand near the head because "it didn't fire". The Parking Lot already said this would happen on stock GRBL; the copy repeated the claim anyway.
