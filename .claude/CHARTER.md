# Kerf — Charter

**Purpose.** A free, open-source GRBL laser cutter app that takes a design made in another tool (SVG, PNG, DXF), puts it on layers, and sends it to the laser safely and correctly, because every open-source tool that tried to match LightBurn's breadth died or became a mess, and a focused one can be done well.
**For.** Lee first, running his own laser. The OSS door stays open for anyone else who designs in Illustrator, Figma or Inkscape and needs that design burned onto material without a commercial license; Community lives in the arc and never outranks Lee's own cutting.
**Done looks like.** Steady state, not a version number: (1) the core loop Import, Trace if raster, Assign to layers, Preview, Send works end to end on Lee's machine with no cut dying mid-job and no silent G-code error; (2) the streaming and safety stack is verified in CI by the GRBL simulator, so a release no longer queues on the owner's hardware; (3) the hardening program's phases are shipped and the owner hardware test card passes on a release build. v1.0 is aspiration, not the finish line.
**Non-goals.** Parametric or full CAD (design in Illustrator, Figma or Inkscape; the built-in drawing tools stay minimal). Firmware beyond GRBL, CNC milling, or 3D modeling and simulation. AI-generated designs and built-in font rendering (trace from PNG instead).
**Phase arc.** Founding build, Feb 2026: CAD/CAM core, G-code engine, tracing, serial safety (e-stop, M5 on abort), gate: the loop runs on Lee's laser. Fortification and cut-reliability/UX revamp, Jun 2026: cut-path audit fixes, layer order, sidebar, first-start freeze, gate: owner confirms cuts on hardware. Hardening and Efficiency Program, Jul 2026 onward: Phase 0 deck clearing, Phase 1 GRBL simulator, comprehensive remediation v0.8.25, gate: owner hardware test card passes and the laser is confirmed back in service (here). Phase 2 streaming rework through Phase 4 geometry (gate D2, Clipper2 is a Lee plus architect call), then v0.9 Camera and Rotary, gate D4: hardware confirmed, parked on Lee's go. v1.0 Profiles, Text and Community, gate D3: parked on Lee's go after the hardening program; silence keeps D3 and D4 parked.
**Drift tripwires.** Scope: has a feature been added that is not on the path Import, Trace, Layers, Preview, Send, or that reintroduces a named exclusion (CAD, non-GRBL firmware, AI design, fonts)? Audience: has work been done that assumes a user other than a person who designs elsewhere and owns a GRBL laser, for example multi-firmware, cloud accounts, or a community feature before gate D3? Built-for-the-builder: has a release shipped tooling, refactors, or test infrastructure for its own sake without a cut on the owner's material getting safer, more correct, or easier?
Founded 2026-02-21. Rewritten only with Lee's approval.
